# Sprout service specification — working draft (8 November 2026)

> INTERNAL DRAFT. Subject to legal and commercial review. Do not publish or represent this as signed customer terms. No database price publication is authorised.

## Product and scope
- **Sprout** is a single responsive landing page with **up to five sections** (not five separate pages).
- An agreed contact/WhatsApp facility and basic SEO are included. Scope, content, deliverables, acceptance criteria and revisions must be recorded and approved in writing before development begins.
- Customer-requested text changes, image replacement, new sections, redesign and additional functionality **after the agreed scope/acceptance** are additional work. A correction to Mogen's defective implementation of the agreed scope is not additional work.
- Mogen may grant a discretionary discount on separately quoted work; this does not establish a continuing entitlement.

## Payment choices
| Option | Amount | Setup fee | Billing/commitment | Hosting / responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Managed Monthly | R199 per month in advance | R0 | Initial six-month commitment (subject to mandatory consumer rights) | Mogen-managed |
| Managed Annually | R1,499 every year | R0 | Annual billing; same scope as Monthly | Mogen-managed |
| Self-Hosted | R1,799 once-off | R0 | Milestone-based project | Customer-managed |

The two managed options provide **identical service**; only the billing interval and allocation period differ. Managed Annually has no separate renewal amount. Prices are draft proposals until explicit publication approval. VAT treatment is unresolved.

### Monthly start and delivery timeline
- On receipt of the first R199 monthly payment in advance, Mogen begins development, without a separate setup or 50% deposit.
- During development, website runs under a temporary **Mogen subdomain**, without corporate email.
- Target review within **3–4 days** of project commencement, subject to receipt of content and feedback.
- Target domain purchase/configuration, final hosting, final deployment, and one corporate mailbox on **day 8**, subject to approval, payment status, domain availability and third-party provisioning. Clarify whether days are calendar or business days before contract publication.
- One mailbox only: customer selects the local part, e.g. info@, help@ or support@, subject to availability and acceptable use. No database.
- The month-one payment is the first monthly subscription payment and not a setup fee.

### Managed Annual and Self-Hosted project commencement
- A **50% advance payment**, credited towards the project/annual price, is required before development starts. For Annual R1,499, proposed rounded payment **R750**; for Self-Hosted R1,799, **R899.50**.
- Balance-payment timing requires agreement (recommend before activation / handover). Any cancellation deductions/refunds must be reasonable and comply with applicable law. Do **not** describe all deposits as automatically non-refundable.

## Domain, hosting and email
- Managed subscriptions include a domain registration/renewal **allowance of up to R200 per year**, independent of top-level domain. Any registrar cost above that is separately quoted and accepted before purchase.
- Mogen selects its service providers from Sive.host, Cloudflare and Vercel where suitable; a customer-supplied/transferred domain does **not** discount the managed subscription. Confirm which provider actually sells each chosen domain extension and which provides registration vs DNS/hosting.
- Managed **1 GB total storage**, shared by website files and **one** corporate email mailbox. No included database. Upgrades or additional mailboxes/space can be separately quoted; Seedling may be more appropriate for larger requirements (up to five mailboxes under its later agreed scope).
- Record domain **registrant identity and registrant rights** separately from Mogen's registrar account, payment and technical management. Do not promise Mogen legal ownership of customer registrant rights contrary to applicable registry rules. Arrange transfers subject to registrant rights, registrar policies and transparent actual work/costs; do not obstruct registrant-initiated transfer based on payment disputes.

## Managed technical maintenance and defect support
- **1 unit = 15 minutes**.
- Managed Monthly: **2 units each month**, up to **2 unused units** may carry into the immediately following month; maximum available in any month **4 units**. Rollover is not cumulative indefinitely.
- Managed Annually: **24 units per subscription year**, available flexibly throughout the year; up to **6 unused units** may carry into the next subscription year (one rollover only, not an indefinitely accumulating credit).
- Technical maintenance includes routine platform upkeep, security/dependency updates, hosting compatibility and operational checks as appropriate. These units are **not** customer-requested content-edit allowances.
- A defect attributable to Mogen's failure to deliver the agreed original functionality must be corrected without fee and **without reducing maintenance credits**.
- Security issues and ordinary compatibility upkeep attributable to the managed solution are Mogen's responsibility, but define response windows, backups and reasonable service limits before publication. Never imply a guaranteed fix for every external outage or third-party defect.

## Additional work
- Changes to approved content, images, layouts, new features, increased scope and requested extras are **out of scope**.
- Proposed price: **R250 per approved support/work request (call-out)** plus **R50 per 15-minute labour unit** (= R200/hour). Clarify remote vs on-site, chargeable minimum, VAT and whether each distinct work order incurs a call-out.
- Work must be described, quoted/estimated and approved in writing before chargeable work begins; urgent security/availability repairs within Mogen's managed obligation are not billed merely because they need labour.
- Mogen may discount additional work at its discretion.

## Renewal and cancellation — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
- Commercial proposal: R400 standard early-cancellation charge for Monthly, R500 for Annually, based loosely on one-third of their initial commitment. These **are proposals or caps, not unconditional automatic forfeitures**. Determine actual reasonable charges with regard to work delivered, avoided costs, amounts already paid and compulsory law.
- No standard cancellation penalty at the agreed end of a fixed period; outstanding earned charges may still apply.
- For CPA section 14 qualifying consumer fixed-term contracts: the consumer may generally cancel on 20 business days' notice, with a reasonable penalty where lawful; notices of expiry and continuation rights apply. **A new fixed-term cycle requires express agreement**; otherwise the statute generally provides month-to-month continuation. Juristic-person transactions have different section 14 applicability. Do not implement blanket six-month/annual automatic fixed-term renewal.
- Managed Annually's annual prepaid continuation/renewal terms and conversion mechanics require separate legal/payment design.
- Self-Hosted: 50% initial payment is a credit against contract price, not automatically non-refundable. On cancellation calculate reasonable delivered work, agreed incurred costs and refunds, subject to law.
- Cancellation, expiry or nonpayment requires explicit domain, email, website suspension and data-export/retention procedures, and reasonable notice.

## Intellectual property, handover and portfolio
- Managed is **website-as-a-service**: Mogen retains its website/source-code rights subject to customer-owned material and third-party licences. Any source-code transfer or buyout is separately negotiated, not automatically included on cancellation.
- Self-Hosted: hand over project source code and files via client Git repository or ZIP following contractual payment conditions. Make explicit which custom deliverables transfer versus Mogen background libraries, reused templates and third-party licences.
- Mogen may showcase agreed non-confidential website design/work in portfolio/marketing, with appropriate client permissions and protections for trademarks, personal data and confidential information.
- Registration of client-branded domains should respect the actual registrant's rights, distinct from source-code ownership and technical administration.
